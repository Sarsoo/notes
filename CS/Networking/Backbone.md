# Tier 1 Nets
- Reach all other networks without paying
	- Settlement-free interconnection
- Tier 2 & 3 pay for interconnects
- Examples
	- AT&T
	- Orange
	- Verizon Enterprise
## Regional Tier 1
- Aren't globally transit-free
	- But tier 1 within a specific region

# Tier 2 Nets
- Pays for some transit
	- Doesn't for others
- Borderline Tier 1
	- RETN
	- Vodafone Carrier
	- Comcast

# Tier 3 Nets
- Pays for all transit

# Hyperscalers
- Challenges tier system
	- Built their own backbones
- Aren't typically considered Tier 1
	- They are transit-free
	- Primary purpose not to sell transit
	- Separate category
