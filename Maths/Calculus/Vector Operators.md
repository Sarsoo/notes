Laplacian
 
- Divergence of the gradient of a function
- $\nabla \cdot \nabla$ , $\nabla^{2}$ , $\Delta$
- Sum of the second partial derivatives w.r.t to each independent variable
- Represents flux density of the gradient flow
 $\Delta f = \sum_{i = 1}^{n} \frac{𝜕^{2} f}{𝜕 x_{i}^{2}}$  

- Where xi is each cartesian coordinate
- It's like double div
	- div-div
