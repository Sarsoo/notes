Types
 
- Passive
	- Omnidirectional
		- Microphone
	- Narrow-beam
		- PIR
			- Passive Infrared
- Active
	- Sonar, radar
	- Emit signal for detecting

Transfer Function
 
- In
	- Physical measured parameter
- Out
	- Usually, electrical output signal
 
- Hardly any are linear
- Multiple inputs
	- Combine for output
- Saturation
	- Output flattens
- Quality
	- Resolution
		- Smallest increment of stimulus
	- Accuracy
		- Distance from truth
	- Precision/Repeatability

Technology
 
- Capacitive
	- Capacitance changes with environment
		- Liquids based on dielectric constant
		- Human body capacitance
	- Displacement, humidity, acceleration, human contact
- Resistive
	- Change in resistance with change in environment
	- Light, force, heat
	- E.g. Camera, street lights, music instruments, weight sensing, touch screen
- Magnetic
	- Passive
	- Detect magnetic fields or alterations of
	- Rotary movement, Earth's magnetic field
	- Hall effect, magneto-diode, magneto-transistor
- Inductive
	- Active
	- Changes in amplitude of high frequency EM field
	- Detecting metal objects
	- Vehicle detection
- Thermoelectric
	- Voltage with differences in temperature
- Pyroelectric
	- Temporary voltage from certain material when heated or cooled
	- Human/animal motion detection, flame detection, NDIR (Non dispersive IR), gas analysis
	- PIR sensors
- Sound level
	- Voltage from vibrations in air
	- Inductive
		- Dynamic mic
	- Capacitive
		- Condenser mic
- Light intensity
	- Current generated from light source
	- Photodiodes
- Electromechanical
	- Mechanical devices
	- Fluid flow
	- Microelectromechanical
		- MEMS gyroscopes
- Electrochemical
	- Electricity & Chemistry
	- CO detector, pH meter
