- Resource with economic value

# Classifications
- Current
	- Short term
	- Expected to be turned into cash or consumed within a year
	- E.g
		- Cash or equivalents
		- Accounts receivable
		- Inventory
		- Prepaid expenses
- Fixed
	- Lifetime greater than a yaar
	- E.g
		- Plants
		- Equipment
		- Buildings
	- Depreciation
		- Cost of asset over time
		- May reflect loss of earning power
- Financial
	- Investments in assets and [securities](Security.md)
	- E.g
		- [Stocks](Stocks.md)
		- Sovereign & corporate bonds
		- Preferred equity
		- Hybrid [securities](Security.md)
- Intangible
	- No physical presence
	- E.g
		- Patents
		- Copyrights
		- Goodwill

# Uses
- Generate cash flow
- Reduce expenses
- Improve sales
