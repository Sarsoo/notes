Sound Pressure Level  
SPL
 
- At 4 kHz
	- Min: 10e-5 Pa
	- Max: 20 Pa
		- Pain
 
- Use $p_{0} = 20  𝜇 P a $ for reference
- Related to amplitude
	- Not 1-to-1
	- Also context and nature
- Frequency dependant
	- Sound with larger amplitude can be quieter
