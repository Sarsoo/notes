- _Directly couple sound energy from eardrum to oval window_
- _Impedance matching sound in air with sound in inner ear fluids_
	- Otherwise would mostly reflect - **Malleus** receives vibrations from sound pressure on drum
	- Connected to drum at longest part by a ligament
	- Manubrium/Handle
	- Head at the top
- Transmits to **incus**
	- Frequency dependent lever arm ratio?
- Transmits to **stapes**
	- Wide base of stapes rests on oval window
	- Transmitted through oval window to cochlea fluid
		- Increasing force but reducing the velocity and displacement, thereby coupling the acoustic energy
      
    
- Ossicles amplification with lever arm factor of 1.3
- Effective vibratory area of drum is 14x larger than oval window
	- Sound pressure concentrated
	- Amplified ~18.1 times

	- Sense of balance

		- **Tympanic**

		- Separates vestibular and cochlear duct
	- **Basilar membrane**
		- Separates cochlear duct from tympanic
		- Frequency analysis
			- High freq at narrow, thin, base end
			- Low freq at wide and thick apex end
	- **Organ of Corti**
		- Sensory epithelium
		- Cellular layer on basilar membrane
		- Powered by potential difference of perilymph and endolymph
