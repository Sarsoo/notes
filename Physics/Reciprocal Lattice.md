1. In real space there is a periodic arrangements of the atoms in a lattice. This is described real space lattice vectors, R.
2. That real space periodicity means there are only certain allowed values of lattice vectors in reciprocal space, G.
3. Allowed values of G have implications for the energy of electrons in the periodic potential.

_Reciprocal space is the Fourier transform of the real space lattice_

Unit Vectors
 
# Real Space

$x ,  y$  

# Reciprocal Space

$k_{x} ,  k_{y}$
