Density of States
 
Concentration of electronic states:

- In a given energy range per unit volume (for 3D)
- In a given energy range per atom
 
- Should be $J^{- 1} m^{- 3}$
	- Actually use $\left(e V\right)^{- 1} \left(c m\right)^{- 3}$

Number of electrons = DOS x occupancy probability
 $$n = \int_{- \infty}^{\infty} D \left(E\right) \cdot f \left(E\right)   d E$$

- 3d
	- Lots of electrons in small energy range
	- Suggests larger DOS
- 4s
	- One electron in large energy range
	- Suggest smaller DOS
