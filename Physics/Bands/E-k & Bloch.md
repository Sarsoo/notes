Bloch's Theorem

$$u_{k} \left(r\right) = u_{k} \left(r + R\right)$$

- $R$ = Lattice vector
- $u$ = Periodic function

E-k relations
 $$E = \frac{\hbar^{2} k^{2}}{2 m^{*}}$$  

- $m \star$ = Effective mass
- Parabolic with k
 
# 1st Derivative -\> Velocity

$$\frac{d E}{d k} = \frac{\hbar^{2} k}{m^{*}}$$  

- $\hbar^{2} k$ = crystal momentum
 $$v = \frac{1}{\hbar} \frac{d E}{d k}$$  

- Velocity from rate-of-change of energy
 
# 2nd Derivative -\> Effective Mass

$$\frac{d^{2} E}{d k^{2}} = \frac{\hbar^{2}}{m^{*}}$$ 
$$m^{*} = \hbar^{2} \left(\frac{d^{2} E}{d k^{2}}\right)^{- 1}$$  

- Effective mass from second derivative of E-k relations
