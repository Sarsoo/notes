# UN Framework Convention on Climate Change

[UNFCCC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Framework_Convention_on_Climate_Change)

- June 1992
	- Rio
	- Earth Summit
- Recognition of CO2 emissions
- Stabilise CO2
	- Ensure ecosystems can adapt
	- Make sure food production not threatened
	- Allow sustainable economic development
- Precise and regular GHG inventories from industrialised countries
- Establishes a framework
- Heavier burden on developed countries
	- Annex 1
		- Industrialised economies and economies in transition
	- Annex 2
		- Richest annex 1
- Developing countries emissions will grow before shrinking
	- Will have largest climate change impacts

# Kyoto

- COP 3
- December 97
- [Kyoto Protocol](https://unfccc.int/kyoto_protocol)
- First implementation of UNFCCC measures
	- Superseded by Paris agreement
- Yanks and Aussies didn't sign up
	- Chinese signed up but don’t have targets and is still increasing
		- Second largest emitter
	- Canadians dropped out in 2012
- Targets
	- Bring developed GHG back to 5% below 1990 levels
		- Over first commitment period
			- Anticipates other commitment periods
				- No duration
				- No goal
	- 37 industrialised EU countries binding emissions targets
	- GHG included CO2, HFCs
- Penalties
	- Reduce emissions by 1.3 units for every unit overshot in subsequent commitment period
- Mechanisms
	- Emissions trading
		- Trade carbon quotas between annex 1 countries
	- Joint implementation
		- Annex 1 invest in emission reduction projects in other A1 countries
			- Rewarded with emissions reduction units
				- ERU
	- Clean development mechanism
		- A1 receive ERUs for emissions reductions in developing countries
- Commitment periods
	- 2008-2012
	- 2013-2020
		- [Doha Amendment](https://unfccc.int/process/the-kyoto-protocol/the-doha-amendment)
