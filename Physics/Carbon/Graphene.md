Fermi Velocity
 
- Considering photons
	- Dispersion relation is proportional to momentum x velocity
- Can we get a velocity in our dispersion relation?
- $a t$ is [length] x [energy]
	- [length]/[time] x [energy] x [time]
		- [length]/[time] = velocity
		- [energy] x [time] = Js = Planck Constant
	- $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} a t = \hbar v_{f}$
		- Where $v_{f}$ is the Fermi velocity of electrons in graphene
