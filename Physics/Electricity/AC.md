- $P$
	- Active Power
		- Real Power
	- Watt (W)
- $Q$
	- Reactive Power
	- Volt-ampere reactive (VAR)
- $S$
	- Complex Power
	- Volt-ampere (VA)
- $|S|$
	- Apparent Power
	- Volt-ampere (VA)
- $\varphi$
	- Phase of voltage relative to current
	- $\varphi=arg(V)-arg(I)$
	- Current lagging voltage (quadrant I vector)
	- Current leading voltage (quadrant IV vector)

# Power Factor
Ratio of real power to apparent power
- Lower PF have higher circulating current
	- Energy back to source from load storage
	- Higher losses
