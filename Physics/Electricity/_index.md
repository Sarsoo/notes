# Required for Current
- Free charges that can move
- Medium to support current
	- Vacuum counts
- Potential difference

# Definitions
## Voltage
- Conventional current flows from higher voltage to lower voltage
## Power
- Electric power, like mechanical power, is the rate of doing work, measured in watts
$$P = work\space done\space per\space unit\space time=\frac{VQ}{t}=VI=I^2R=\frac{V^2}{R}$$
## Loads
Passive
- Charges move through PD from high to low
	- Work is done by charges on device
- Potential energy of the charges due to voltage between terminals
	- Converted to kinetic energy
## Sources
Active
- Charges move through device from low to high
	- Work done on charge
- Energy converted to electric potential from other type
	- Mechanical
	- Chemical
- Generators
- Batteries
