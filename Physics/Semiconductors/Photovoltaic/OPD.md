Performance Parameters
 
- Quantum efficiency
	- Ratio of number of charge carriers collected to number of incident photons
	- $E Q E \left(\lambda\right) = \frac{I_{p h}}{P} = \frac{h c}{\lambda q}$
- Responsivity
	- Ratio of photocurrent at wavelength to power of incident photon of wavelength
	- $R \left(\lambda\right) = \frac{I_{p h}}{P}$
	- $I_{p h} = \left(I_{l i g h t} - I_{d a r k}\right)$
- Noise and noise equivalent power
	- NEP
		- Minimum incident power of photons required to generate photocurrent equal to noise at specific frequency
		- Optical power when SNR is unity
		- $N E P \left(\lambda\right) = \frac{I_{n}}{R \left(\lambda\right)}$
- Response time
- Detectivity
	- Sensitivity of active layer
	- Instead of specific device
	- Area dependent
	- Allows comparison of photodiodes of different area
	- $D e t e c t i v i t y \left(D\right) = \frac{\sqrt{A}}{N E P}$
