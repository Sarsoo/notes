	- One electron-hole pair excited per photon
	- Thermal relaxation of the pair energy in excess of the band gap
	- Unconcentrated solar light (AM1.5) at 6000 K blackbody spectrum
- Results
	- S-Q limit is the maximum theoretical efficiency of single PN solar cell
	- Max efficiency is about 33.7%
		- For a single PN junction with BG = 1.34 eV and AM1.5

# Loss Mechanisms

- Blackbody radiation
	- 7% of available incoming energy
	- As cell heats up, more BB radiation
		- Higher than 7%
- Radiative recombination
	- Opposite of intended process
	- Electron-hole recombine and produce photon
- Spectrum losses
	- Most significant
	- Need energy higher than bandgap-defined cut-off
		- Accounts for 19% in Si
			- 19% of AM1.5 light is lower energy than Si's bandgap
	- Excess $E_{p h}$ above $E_{g}$ not useful
		- Kinetic energy of the free electron
		- Absorbed as heat
		- 33% of incident sunlight
- Theoretical conversion limit of about 48%

Quantum Efficiency
 
- Probability of an incident photon of energy E delivering one electron to the external circuit
- Depends on
	- Absorption coefficient of solar cell material
	- Efficiency of charge separation
	- Efficiency of charge collection
- Not dependent on incident spectrum

# IQE

$I Q E \left(E\right) = \frac{E Q E \left(E\right)}{1 - R - T}$  

- Internal
- Collected charge carriers to absorbed photons
	- 1 - R - T for absorption
