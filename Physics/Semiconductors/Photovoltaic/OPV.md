Motivation
 
- Higher light absorption
	- Low reflection
- Efficiency
	- Optimal band gap
		- Balance Isc/Voc
	- Shockley-Queisser limit
		- 33% max efficiency for 1.3-1.4 eV
- Device bending/flexing
- Low cost & efficient fabrication

	- High throughput

Excitons
 
- Bound e-h pairs
	- Neutral excited state species
	- Not free pairs
	- Will be confined to a molecule
		- Frenkel exciton
	- Also negative/positive polaron
- Need to split
- Why?
	- Weak VDW forces
		- Narrow energy level band width
		- Spatially restricts wave function
	- Attractive coulombic potential well
		- Extends over large volume
		- Felt for longer distances
		- Because of low dielectric constant
- Soft lattice
