Polycrystalline
 
- Want more optimal band gap
- Too expensive to grow single crystalline
- Microcristallites or ‘grains’ with random orientation
- Boundaries create defects and trap states
	- Charge transport affected when crossing boundary
		- Reduced majority carrier mobility
	- Minority carrier recombination increases
		- Dark current increases
- Performance is carrier density dependent
 
# CIGS

- Copper indium diselenide
- Only grows as p-type
- Tuneable band gap 1.04-1.67 eV
- Efficiency up to 22%
 
# CdTe

- Grows as p-type
- II-VI
- Bandgap 1.44 eV
- Efficiency 16-18%
- High trap density
	- Grain boundaries
	- High dark current
		- 20 x GaAs
	- Low Voc
		- 0.2 V less than GaAs

Amorphous
 
- Lower cost
- Large area production on various substrates
- Amorphous Si
	- Higher absorption coefficient than monocrystalline
		- Thinner films needed
	- 1.7 eV bandgap
	- Poor mobility
	- Defects/dangling bonds
	- Not as high conversion efficiency
	- Problems
		- Small Voc
		- Poor blue light response
		- Degradation

Si
 
- c-Si
	- Carriers formed in bulk
	- Diffuse to junction
- a-Si
	- Too many defects
		- Carriers must be swept out by field
	- Drift device is required to sweep out carriers
		- Intrinsic region, with built in field
		- Due to p and n doped regions
