$n = N_{c} \cdot e^{\left(- \left(E_{c} - E_{F}\right)\right)/\left(k T\right)}$  
$p = N_{v} \cdot e^{\left(- \left(E_{F} - E_{v}\right)\right)/\left(k T\right)}$  

- $E_{C}$ is the position of the conduction band minimum
- $E_{V}$ is the position of the valence band maxmimum
- $k$ = Boltzmann's constant
- $N_{x}$ are the effective density of states

$n p = n_{i}^{2}$  

- $n_{i}$ = instrinsic carrier concentration
 $n_{i} = \sqrt{N_{c} N_{v}} e^{\left(\left(- E\right)_{g}\right)/\left(2 k t\right)}$  

- $E_{g}$ = band gap = $E_{c} - E_{v}$

Substitutional Doping
 
- Donated electrons are delocalised
- Ions are immobile

$N_{c} \equiv 2 \left[\frac{2 \pi m_{n} k T}{h^{2}}\right]^{3/2}$  
$N_{v} \equiv 2 \left[\frac{2 \pi m_{p} k T}{h^{2}}\right]^{3/2}$
