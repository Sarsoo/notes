Singlet/Triplet Excitons
 
- Combine electron and hole
	- Form a photon
		- No spin
			- Can add both in a way that has resultant spin
				- Won't produce light
- 1:3
	- Singlet state
		- Fluorescence emission
	- Triplet state
		- Radiative decay spin-forbidden
		- Decay non-radiatively
- To increase interactions that emit light
	- Spin-orbit coupling enhanced
	- Phosphorescence
