OLED
 
- Electrons from cathode
	- Low work function
- Holes from anode
	- High work function
- Balanced injection for efficient emission
- Small injection barrier for low voltage operation
 ![Exported image](../../../img/OneNote/Charge%20Injection%20image%20282831354921a118.png)

OFET
 
- Ideally flat band condition
	- Not always possible due to mismatch between metal work function (Fermi level) and semiconductor energy levels
- Move work function closer to HOMO or LUMO depending on carrier to be injected
	- Move to HOMO to inject holes

# [Polarons](../Organic.md)
 
- Slightly different energy levels
	- Injection occurs into these levels
- P+ and P- for positive and negative polarons
- Polaron binding energy
	- Difference between polaron level and undisturbed

- Electrode has high work function
- Need delta1 energy to inject electrons into P- level
- Need delta2 energy to inject holes into P+ level

Ohmic
 
- Does not limit current
- Linear and symmetric I-V characteristics
- Constant resistance
	- Gradient of Ohm's law

# Schottky Barriers

- Potential barrier at metal-semiconductor interface
	- Can be used as diode
- Mismatch in energy levels between contacts and semiconductors
	- n-type semiconductor with smaller work function than metal
	- p-type semiconductor with larger work function than metal

## Minimising Barrier

- Change metal electrode
	- Different work function
- Apply polar SAM
- Insert intermediate layer with smaller barrier
	- PEDOT polymer in OLEDs and OPV
		- Hole injecting/collecting layer
