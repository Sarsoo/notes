Charge Carrier Density
 
- FET channel
	- $Q = n \cdot q \cdot d_{a c c} \cdot A$
	- Charge density, unit charge, volume
	- $d_{a c c}$ = accumulation layer thickness
- Estimate from capacitance
	- $Q = C_{0} \cdot V \cdot A$
	- $C_{0}$ capacitance per unit area
	- $V = V_{g} - V_{t h}$

Comparisons
 
|   |   |
|---|---|
|# Organic|# Inorganic|
|Accumulation regime only|Inversion or in depletion|
|Charged injected from contacts to form channel|Doped channels, carriers already there|
|Contacts are often metals, high contact resistance|Doped semiconductor contacts, low contact resistance|
|Can be made at low temperature and on flexible substrates|Fabricated at very high temperatures|
