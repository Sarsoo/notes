Thermal Deposition
 
- Small molecules
 
1. Evaporation/sublimation of molecules from hot source
2. Transport of molecules through vacuum toward substrate
3. Condensation of molecules on substrate

Molecular Beam Deposition
 
- Similar process to thermal deposition
	- Smaller aperture
	- Forms beam
- Ability to co-deposit different materials
- Monitor using IR laser and e-gun
- Advanced temperature control
- Better control over deposition

Sputtering
 
- Mostly for metal films
- Evacuate to vacuum
- Form plasma using DC or AC
	- 100 - 1000 V
- Fast
- Rate tuneable
	- Target voltage
	- Current density
	- Chamber pressure
- Good for metals and inorganic dielectrics
	- Not on top of other semiconductor layers
		- K.E of sputtered ions is high
		- Plasma in chamber can create UV photons that damage organic materials
