Concentrated Photovoltaics
 
- Reduce blackbody radiation
- Conversion with smaller area
	- Higher efficiency
		- Logarithmic enhancement in operating voltage
	- Area cost is lower
- Slightly higher photon conversion efficiency
- Requires
	- Sun tracking
	- Thermal management
- Concentration
	- Low
		- 2 - 100 suns
	- Medium
		- 100 - 300 suns
	- High
		- \> 1,000 suns
 ![5](../../img/OneNote/Photovoltaic%20image%201c8fa27034fb3fcb.png)  

- Incident angle of unconcentrated sun ~0.26 degrees
	- Absorb from small angle
	- Radiate over 4 pi
- Concentration increases absorption area

MEG  
Multiple Exciton Pair
 
- Reduce spectral losses
- Multiple excited electron-hole pairs generated from a single photon
- Quantum dots
	- Not possible in bulk
- Highly tuneable
- Photon of 2Eg allows excitation of 2 electrons
	- Quantum efficiency exceeding 100%
 
_The quantum mechanical origin of MEG can be described by Impact ionisation: light excites a high-energy exciton which decays irreversibly into a quasi-continuum of multiexciton states available at this energy_
