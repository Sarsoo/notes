Polaron
 
- Quasi-particle for modelling electrons interactions with atoms
	- Extended to include interactions between electrons and ions in metals to produce a bound state
- Lowers the electron's mobility and increases effective mass
- Charge placed in polarisable medium will be screened
	- Local polarisation around charge centre
- Charge carrier together with induced polarisation known as polaron

Gaussian Disorder
 
- Highly localised carriers on individual molecules
- No bands
	- Carriers tunnel from molecule to molecule
		- Or between conjugated sections
- Low mobility
- Energy level ranges
	- Spatial disorder
		- Range of different intermolecular distances and orientations
	- Energetic disorder
		- Range of energy states associated with chain conformations and lengths
