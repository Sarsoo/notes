Production
 
- Electrolysis
	- Can be done with renewable energy
	- Dissociate water into hydrogen and oxygen
	- Can be \> 80% efficient
	- PEM electrolyser
		- Polymer electrolyte membrane
		- Solid electrolyte
		- Water reacts at anode
			- Oxygen and H+ (Protons)
		- Electrons flow through external circuit
			- Hydrogen ions selectively move across PEM to cathode
		- Hydrogen ions combine with electrons at cathode
			- Hydrogen gas
		- ![Anode Reaction. 2H20 02 4H 4e Cathode Reaction 4H ...](../../../img/OneNote/Fuel%20Cells%20image%20819bba03687230f2.png)
- Gasification of coal
	- Produce hydrogen and CO directly
		- Syngas
	- CO reacts with steam
		- Produce more hydrogen
		- Water-gas shift
	- Integrated gasification combined-cycle
		- IGCC
		- Generating electricity from coal with CO2 capture
		- 40% efficiency
			- 34% efficiency for existing electricity production
				- Without carbon capture
		- Modification of process allows coproduction of hydrogen with electricity and CO2
			- Weighting between can be tuned

Storage
 
- Compressed gas
	- 10,000 psi
	- 700 bar
		- Atmospheres
	- Significant amount of space
	- 5% storage losses
- Liquefaction
	- -253 C
		- Energy inefficient
		- Lose 30-40% of energy
	- Slightly more than half the volume of gas
- Solid state
	- Chemically bind to hydrogen
	- Physically adsorb
	- Competing requirements
		- High hydrogen density
		- Reversibility of release/charge at realistic temperatures
			- 70-100 C
		- Fast release/charge kinetics with minimal energy barriers
	- Nanoporous structures
		- Borohydride
			- NH4BH4
			- 24% hydrogen mass ratio

DMFC  
Direct Methanol FC
 
- Same electrolyte as PEMFC
- Platinum ruthenium catalyst
- Advantages
	- Easier transport/storage of methanol
	- Easier water and thermal management
- Disadvantages
	- Produces carbon dioxide
	- Low electro-activity for methanol
	- Reduced efficiency and power
 ![cath0de 12 2 overalk 32 02 240](../../../img/OneNote/Fuel%20Cells%20image%20b8ae39357c0e26ef.png)  

SOFC  
Solid Oxide Fuel Cells
 
- Solid electrolyte
	- Nonporous Y2O3 stabilised ZrO2
- High operating temperature
	- Internal reforming
	- Rapid kinetics without precious metals
		- No catalyst
	- High quality heat cogeneration
- Advantages
	- 2 phase interface
	- Reduced corrosion, electrolyte management issues
- Configurations
	- Tubular seal-less design
	- Bipolar (flat plate)
