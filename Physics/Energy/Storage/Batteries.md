Li-Ion
 
- One or more electrochemical cells
	- Connected in series or parallel
- Anode
	- Electropositive electrode
	- Electrons generated
	- Lithium ions held within graphite
- Cathode
	- Negative electrode
	- Positive ions migrate to
	- Electrons migrate to through external circuitry
- Electrolyte
	- Liquid solution containing salt dissolved in solvent
	- Allows flow of positive ions from one electrode to other
	- Only flow of ions not electrons
- Current collectors
	- Allow transport of electrons to and from electrodes
	- Metals
	- Must not react with electrode materials
- Cell voltage determined by energy of chemical reactions
