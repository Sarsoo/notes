[Earthing - IET](https://electrical.theiet.org/media/1549/earthing.pdf)

Key: T = Terre (earth), N = neutral, C = combined, S = separate
# TN-S
- Neutral connected to earth at supply only
- Max Ze 0.8Ω
# TN-C-S
- Protective Multiple Earthing (PME)
- Supply neutral connected to earth at source **and at intervals along the run**
- Max Ze 0.35Ω
# TT
- Installation has own earth electrode local to the install
- Distributor doesn't provide an earth
## TN-C
- Uncommon
- Requires exemption from Electrical Safety, Quality & Continuity Regulations
# IT
- Uncommon
- Not permitted for Low Voltage public supply
	- Source is not directly earthed
