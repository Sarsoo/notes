# MNIST
- 70,000 hand-drawn characters from US mail
- 28x28 images
- 10 classes (0 through 9)
- Achieved 99.83%
	- Ciresan et al. 2011

# CIFAR-10
- 60,000 colour images
- 32x32 images
- 10 classes
	- Airplane
	- Automobile
	- Bird
	- Cat
	- Deer
	- Dog
	- Frog
	- Horse
	- Ship
	- Truck
- Achieved 90.7%
	- Wan et al. 2013
