Battery storage power station
 
- Short-term peak power
	- Ancillary services
- Reserve power in case of failure
	- Reduce power outages
	- Basically big UPS
- Stored or emitted in DC
	- Usually need AC
		- Inverters
		- GTO thyristors
- 80s
	- Lead-acid batteries
- Next decades
	- Nickel-Cadmium and Sodium-Sulphur
- \> 2010
	- Li-ion
		- Price falling from car industry
	- Flow batteries?

# Operation

- No movement
	- Quick spin up
- Peak shaving
- Dampen fast oscillations when power networks at full load

# Chemistries

- Li-ion
	- Hornsdale Power Reserve, Tesla, Australia
		- 100 MW
		- Samsung 21-70-size cells
- Sodium-Sulphur
	- Buzen, Fukuoka Prefecture, Japan
		- 300 MWh
		- 50 MW
		- Stabilise fluctuations from renewables
- Lithium Iron Phosphate
	- Hong Kong
		- 40 MWh
		- 20MW
		- 60,000 Lithium Iron Phosphate cells
			- 230 Ah each
- Lead-acid
	- Notrees, Texas
		- 36 MW for 40 minutes
- Nickel-Cadmium
	- Golden Valley Electric, Fairbank
		- One of the largest
		- 25 MW, 15 minutes
		- 13,760 batteries in 4 strands
