Amplification
 
- Signal repeater
- Solid-state amplifier
	- Typically Erbium-doped fibre amplifier
- Each repeater contains separate equipment for each fibre
- Responsible for:
	- Signal reforming
	- Error measurement
	- Controls
- Also for [wavelength division multiplexing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavelength-division_multiplexing)
	- Increases capacity
- Powered by DC passed down conductor near centre of cable
	- All in series
	- Fed by stations at either end
		- Share generation, one + P.D., one - P.D.
		- Virtual earth halfway along
		- 3 - 15 kV DC
		- \< 1.1 A
		- Current at 10 kV DC is up to 1,650 mA
		- \< 16.5 kW
- Can have 100 km between repeaters
 
- Pre and post-amplification

Cable Types
 
# Unrepeated

- Preferred in short cable routes
	- No repeaters
	- Lower cost
	- Caps max distance
- PCSF
	- Pure silica core
	- Low loss
		- 0.172 dB per km at 1550 nm
	- Larger chromatic dispersion
		- Specific Tx/Rx equipment
		- Can reduce interference between channels in single fibre
			- Wavelength division multiplexing (WDM)
				- Limited by optical bandwidth of amplifiers
				- Limited by spacing between frequencies of optical carriers
					- Minimum often is 50 GHz (0.4nm)
				- Can reduce max length

# Locating Faults

- Shore station
	- Electrical measurements
	- [Spread-spectrum time-domain reflectometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spread-spectrum_time-domain_reflectometry) (SSTDR)
		- Can be used in live environments quickly
			- Complete dataset in 20ms
 
# Repairing

- Boat drops marker near fault
- Grapple used to retrieve cable
	- Different profile for different bed types
		- Sand/Rock
- Deep water
	- Cable may not be strong enough to lift all the way
	- Cut cable soon after capture
	- One length of cable brought to surface at a time
		- New section spliced in
	- New cable longer
		- Laid in U shape on bed
- Submersible used for shallower waters
