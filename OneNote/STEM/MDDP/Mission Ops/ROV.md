TMS  
Tether Management System
 
- Garage-like device containing ROV during **splash zone**
- Separate assembly on top of ROV
	- For larger work-class ROVs
 
- Lengthen and shorten cable to minimise effect of cable drag
	- Underwater currents
- [Forum Energy Technology - TMS (Most failures due to TMS)](https://f-e-t.com/subsea/vehicles/tether-management-systems/)

Umbilical Cord
 
- Armoured Cable
	- Electrical conductors
		- Power
	- Fibre optics
		- Video
		- Data signals
 
- High-power applications
	- Power drives motor driving hydraulic pump
		- Propulsion
		- Power equipment
			- Torque tools
			- Manipulator arms

_"By placing the light components on the top and the heavy components on the bottom, the overall system has a large separation between the centre of buoyancy and the_ _centre of gravity__: this provides stability and the stiffness to do work underwater"_
 \> From \<[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remotely_operated_underwater_vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remotely_operated_underwater_vehicle)\>  

Configuration
 
- **Open or Box Frame**
	- Open frame where mechanical components enclosed
	- Free-swimming in light currents
		- \< 4 knots
	- Not for towing
		- Poor hydrodynamics
	- Work-class and heavy work-class
 
- **Torpedo**
	- Data gathering/Inspection
	- Low hydrodynamic resistance
		- Control limitations
	- Needs high speed
		- Positionally and attitudinally stable
	- _"Tow fish"_

Classification
 
- Micro
	- Can be \< 3 kg
	- Alternative to a diver
		- Where environment to small
- Mini
	- ~ 15 kg
	- Diver alternative
	- Can be deployed and used by a single person
	- _"Eyeball-class"_
		- Not for intervention
- General
	- \< 5 HP
	- Can have small 3 finger grippers
	- May have sonar
	- Light survey applications
	- \< 1,000m depth
- Inspection Class
	- Rugged commercial or industrial
	- Observation and data gathering
		- Fitted with many sensors
	- Can also have manipulators and grippers for light work
- Light Work-class
	- \< 50 HP
	- Can have manipulators
	- May have polymer-based chassis
	- \< 2000m depth
- Heavy Work-class
	- \< 220 HP
	- Can have at least 2 manipulators
	- \< 3,500m depth
- Trenching & Burial
	- 220 HP \<  
		\< 500 HP
	- Can carry cable carrying sled
	- Work at 6,000m
