Embodied Carbon
 
- Carbon footprint of a material
- Amount of GHG released through supply chain
	- Greenhouse gases
	- Often from cradle to factory gate
	- Or Cradle to site (of use)
- Includes
	- Extraction of materials from ground
	- Transport
	- Refining
	- Processing
	- Assembly
	- In-use
		- For cradle to grave
- Cannot be reversed
	- Done when emitted
