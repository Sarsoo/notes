Diesel-Electric
 
_"The defining characteristic of diesel-electric transmission is that it avoids the need for a_ _gearbox__, by converting the_ _mechanical force_ _of the diesel engine into_ _electrical energy_ _(through a_ _dynamo__), and using the electrical energy to drive_ _traction motors__, which propel the vehicle mechanically"_
 
- Power directly or from batteries
- Sometimes called electric transmission

Azimuth Thrusters
 
- 360° rotation for manoeuvrability
- No need for rudder
- **Mechanical Transmission**
	- Connects to motor by gearing
	- L-drive
		- Vertical input / horizontal output
	- Z-drive
		- Horizontal input / vertical rotating column/ horizontal output
	- Fixed, **retractable** or underwater-mountable
		- Retractable used as auxiliary propulsion for dynamic positioning
		- Underwater-mountable used for dynamic positioning on huge vessels
	- Fixed or controllable pitch propellers
- **Electrical Transmission**
	- "Pods"
	- Electric motor inside pod connected to propeller
	- Electricity from **diesel** or **gas turbine**
- _"The most powerful podded thrusters in use are the four 21.5 MW Rolls-Royce Mermaid units fitted to RMS Queen Mary 2"_
- Increase draught

DC Distribution
 
_"This concept represents a new way of distributing energy for low-voltage installations on ships. It can be used for any electrical ship application up to 20 megawatts and operates at a nominal voltage of 1000 V DC. The DC distribution system is simply an extension of the multiple DC links that already exist in all propulsion and thruster drives, which usually account for more than 80 percent of the electrical power_  
_consumption on electric propulsion vessels"_  
[DC Distribution System (Ship Propulsion) - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_distribution_system_\(ship_propulsion\))
 
_"The efficiency improvement is mainly achieved from the system no longer being locked at a specific frequency (usually 60 Hz on ships), even though a 60 Hz power source can also be connected to the grid. This new freedom of being able to control each power source totally independently opens up numerous ways of optimizing fuel consumption."_
 
_"One comparison using the DC distribution system instead of the traditional AC system for a_ _Platform Supply Vessel_ _(PSV), reduced the weight of the electrical system components from 115,520 kilograms (254,680 lb) to 85,360 kilograms (188,190 lb). Another saves 15-30% fuel."_
 
_"The biggest potential for fuel savings lies in the ease with which energy storage devices, such as batteries or super capacitors, can be added to the system. Energy storage will help the engines level out load variations from the thrusters and other large loads."_
 
- Challenges mostly safety based
